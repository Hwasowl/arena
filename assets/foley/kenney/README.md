# Recorded foley sources

These short recordings are from Kenney's [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) and [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) packs. Both packs are published under [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Credit is optional; we credit Kenney here to preserve provenance.

Files prefixed `impact` and `footstep` came from Impact Sounds. `metalClick`, `metalLatch`, `drawKnife`, `knifeSlice`, and `cloth` files came from RPG Audio. They were converted from the original OGG files to 48 kHz, 16 bit, mono WAV without other edits. `tools/make-skill-audio.py` layers the recorded attacks with synthetic fantasy tails.

The files here are source material for the generated clips in `assets/skill-pack-v1/sounds/`. Do not put these source recordings in the browser bundle separately.
